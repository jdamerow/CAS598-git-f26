The traveler knew this stranger meant bad news. In fact, there is something unsettlingly familiar about him...

The phone, is too close to the stranger, making it not an option. The trapdoor is of course suspicious, but options are limited and there is probably a reason it cliked just as the stranger entered. One thing at a time.

The traveller quickly throws back the rug, opens the trapdoor, and jumps in without even groping for a ladder. The traveler has little time to think as they plummet through the darkness and then quickly come to a stop as they hit the wet earth below. It is pitch black in here, and it smells somewhere between a marsh and a cave.
