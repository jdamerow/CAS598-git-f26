The rain beat against the glowing sign of the *Blue Heron Diner*, blurring its light into the dark forest road. Inside, the place was empty except for a traveler sitting in the back booth, stranded after their car battery mysteriously died down the road.

On the laminate table sat a heavy silver pocket watch. It didn't belong to them, yet it had appeared on the car's dashboard when the engine failed. Its hands spun wildly backward, ignoring the normal flow of time.

Suddenly, three things happened at once.

A drenched stranger wearing a heavy coat stepped into the diner, clutching a satchel bound in iron chains. At the same moment, the old payphone on the back wall began to ring loudly. Beneath the traveler's boots, a faint click echoed as a hidden trapdoor unlocked under the floor rug.

The waitress behind the counter didn't look up from wiping the stove. "Decision time," she muttered. "You can't sit in the middle forever."
