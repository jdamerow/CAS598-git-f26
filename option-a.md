As a human wirter who has been writing for eight years. I simply cannot engage with a story already partially written by AI. Therefore, we shall start from the beginning instead.

This story has nothing to do with a watch or a man in a Blue Heron Diner or a choice

This story starts with demons and this story will end with demons.

There were also creatures with long crooked teeth and gnawing fangs and ghostly permeability and a lust for blood.

But these are not the demons of the story. These are Hollow Spawn. 

Control over Hollow Spawn meant control over the lands. And none had more control than the House of Ash.

The world would burn and languish under their sickly watch. The world would be torn asunder by their brutality. Of course, there was nothing to be done. The House of Ash were the very best at one skill--the most important skill. Their control over Hollow Spawn was unmatched. The partnerships made with these creatures almost beautiful. People stopped believing they were human at all, but rather a new type of hollow spawn disguised as man. How else could they be so efficient? So powerful? So cruel?

And so the world waited as the source of all power waned. The age of the House of Ash would come to an end. In its place, Hollow Spawn would remain the dominant force, its users at the forefront of military operations as whole countries fractured off the remnants of the Ashen Kingdom. Each country vied for the same power, the same skill in taming Hollow Spawn. Each house wanted to be the House of Ash. A house of legends. 

But the world would tremble as the fall of the house of Ash had not been thorough enough.
