Fearful of the mysterious stranger and wary of where the trapdoor leads, the traveler hesitantly approaches the ringing payphone. Although the traveler was never much for superstition, they fervently hope for some sign as to what they should do.

Picking up the receiver, a gravely voice on the other side spoke through the static. “Trust no one,” the voice said. Before the traveler had a chance to respond, the line went dead.

Turning back toward the diner, the traveler noticed a curious look on the waitress’s face. In fact, it seems as though all of the occupants in the diner were looking expectantly at the traveler.

Trying to avoid rousing suspicions, the traveler placed a twenty-dollar bill on the counter before briskly exiting back into the rain. Without so much as a backward glance, the traveler disappeared into the darkness.
